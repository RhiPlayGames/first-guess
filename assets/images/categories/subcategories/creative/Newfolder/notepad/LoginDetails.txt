Step 1: Define your IP as Internal Traffic
Go to Admin and under Data collection and modification, click Data streams.
Select your web data stream for scrappytotherescue.com.
Click Configure tag settings > Show more > Define internal traffic.
Click Create, enter a name (e.g., "My Home Office"), and enter your IP address in the value field.
Step 2: Create a Filter to Exclude that Traffic
In Admin, under Data collection and modification, click Data filters.
Click Create Filter and choose Internal Traffic.
Set the filter to Exclude.
Set the filter state to Testing first. This allows you to verify it is working in your reports before it permanently removes your data.
Click Create.
